Turn leftover event food into meals for people who need it

Weddings, conferences and festivals often end with good food going to waste. FoodBridge connects donors with nearby orphanages, shelters and community kitchens, and a volunteer carries it across before it spoils.

1. Donor posts

Dish, portions, cooked time and pickup point on the map. An AI-style freshness estimator sets a safe-until time.

2. Receiver claims

An AI planner (OR-Tools) splits big donations across several receivers by room, distance, veg / non-veg preference and who hasn't eaten today. Each receiver accepts or declines its share.

3. Volunteer delivers

Each accepted share gets the nearest free volunteer, who moves it from pickup to drop-off.

Built with open source: Flask, SQLite, OR-Tools, Leaflet, OpenStreetMap, OSRM and Nominatim.
